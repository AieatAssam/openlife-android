OpenLife helps you keep a private record of the files you choose to save.

Your originals stay on your device. OpenLife does not use cloud processing,
accounts, sync, telemetry, or automatic sharing. It does not back up your
OpenLife data: if the app data or device storage is cleared, the data is gone.

The project is being built capability by capability. The available workflow
and supported file types are intentionally limited while the app is under
development.
